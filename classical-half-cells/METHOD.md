# Search method and verification boundary

This job uses Smith's Proposition 5.15 and Theorem 1.5, with the potential sign convention in PROOF.md. The selected auxiliary polynomials can have nonreal roots. Their degrees limit the search pool; the resulting certificate proves the integral condition for every nonzero integer polynomial. It makes no use of an optimizer characterization that assumes real-rooted auxiliaries.

The original density cone has 1,510 independent positive source hats from Tomar's public adaptive-node certificate, plus a broad unit-mass triangle. Both retained CG and classical searches use this identical cone, identical source nodes and identical numerical margins. The triangle has zero mass in the checked coarse artifacts. The piecewise-linear density is exported on exactly the same 2,338 rational nodes in both cases.

For half-cell refinement, every source interval is subdivided at its exact rational midpoint. Positive interpolated source heights select 3,068 independent unit-mass hats. Original densities can be represented exactly as nonnegative combinations of these refined hats, so the cone is nested. The source heights select variables; they do not fix the optimized heights. The paired CG and classical full refined searches have identical allowed density variables and numerical margins.

The floating LP minimizes the first moment, with mass one, nonnegative hat masses and polynomial weights, potential slack 2e-6 at its current cuts, direct auxiliary logarithmic integrals at least 1e-7, and weighted degree at most 1-1e-8. Root-based logarithmic evaluation handles complex roots and leading coefficients. A dense scan proposes local minima, then 32 ternary-search steps locate continuous cuts. The stopping threshold is 1.85e-6. This numerical stage cannot certify a bound.

The unchanged inherited search solves each LP from scratch. The incremental adapter keeps all existing rows and the HiGHS simplex basis when adding cuts, returning duals in caller order. A separate density-column pilot begins with the original hats and appends negatively priced refined hats. Its reduced cost is c-U^T nu-IL^T gamma-lambda, including the mass and logarithmic-integral duals. The full refined model provides the main paired comparison; an unfinished column pilot is not an additional bound.

Hat masses are rounded to rational multiples of 1e-14, polynomial weights are rounded downward to that denominator, and density heights, mass and trace are assembled exactly. The declared bound is the exact ratio T/m. A completed candidate is copied to a SHA256-named read-only artifact before either verifier runs.

The first verifier, verify_linear.py, is unchanged from c86r2. The reviewer-written independent_verify.py and endpoint auditor are unchanged from ../review_c86; provenance hashes are recorded. They import no search code. The independent implementation checks exact rational structure, FLINT factorization, certified root balls, direct antiderivative integrals cross-checked against slope jumps, and continuous potential inequalities on every closed support cell. VERIFIER_NOTES.md derives its Taylor and integrable-log remainder bounds. parallel_verify.py only distributes those same interval tests; an exact assembly audit checks all original support cells and endpoint adjacency. Calibration reproduced the sequential run's entire coverage file byte for byte. audit_artifact.py binds all hashes and rejects four corrupted partitions. This is independently written AI verification, not human peer review or a formal proof assistant certificate.
